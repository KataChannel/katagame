## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS (SUMMARY)

### Top Revenue Drivers
1. **Battle Pass** (35% of revenue) - recurring, high take rate
2. **Gacha Cosmetics** (25%) - collection psychology drives spending
3. **Gem Packages** (20%) - primary conversion mechanic
4. **Guilds** (10%) - social features drive engagement & spending
5. **Events** (5%) - seasonal spikes & FOMO mechanics

### Critical Success Factors
1. **Retention** - D7 retention must be 40%+ for monetization
2. **Free-to-play balance** - cosmetics only, no P2W
3. **Multiple payment methods** - essential for Vietnam market
4. **Daily engagement** - login streaks, daily quests drive revenue
5. **Seasonal content** - keeps players coming back

### Risk Mitigation Priorities
1. Ensure retention targets hit before monetizing heavily
2. Have payment backup plans (if one gateway fails)
3. Plan content calendar 2-3 months ahead
4. Monitor churn weekly, act within 2 weeks
5. Build community early (before monetization)
