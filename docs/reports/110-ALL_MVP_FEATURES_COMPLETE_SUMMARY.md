## 🏘️ UPGRADE SYSTEM (NEW)

### 3-Dimension Upgrade Per Province

Every province has **3 independent upgrade tracks** (all MVPs):

1. **Farmer Level** (1-20): Population & basic resources
2. **Resource Level** (1-10): Specific resource specialization
3. **Development Level** (1-15): Infrastructure & overall growth

**Hidden Milestone Benefits**:
- ✅ Passive Bonuses: Automatic resource boosts
- ✅ Active Bonuses: Daily random multipliers (x2/x3/x5 for 1 hour)
- ✅ Synergy Bonuses: Related resources boost each other

### Daily Historical Stories

**MVP1**: 30 stories (first 30 days)
- Day 1: "Hùng Vương Lập Nước" (Nation founding)

- Day 4: "Lúa Việt" (Vietnamese rice)
- Day 5: "Văn Hóa Tết" (Tet culture)
- Days 6-30: Mix of historical, geographical, cultural stories

**Story Features** (All MVPs):
- 5-10 minute reads
- Educational content
- Historical accuracy
- Cultural focus
- Geographic information

**Quiz System** (All MVPs):
- 3 questions per story
- Comprehension, Context, Application
- Scoring: 0-3 correct
- Multipliers: ×1, ×2, ×3, ×5 (perfect bonus)

**Rewards** (All MVPs):
- Base: 100 Gold + 100 Rice + 50 Wood
- Perfect (3/3): ×5 multiplier (1,250 resources)
- Partial (2/3): ×3 multiplier

**Content Growth**:
