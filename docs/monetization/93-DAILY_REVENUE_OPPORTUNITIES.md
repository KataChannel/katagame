```
├─ ARPPU (Average Revenue Per Paying User)
├─ Battle Pass take rate: % of DAU with Premium Pass
├─ Gacha conversion: % attempting gacha pulls
└─ Session length: Minutes per session (correlates to spending)

Weekly Metrics:
├─ 7-day revenue (simple sum)
├─ Week-over-week growth %
├─ New payer acquisition
├─ Lost payers (churn)
└─ Revenue per user cohort

Monthly Metrics:
├─ Total monthly revenue
├─ MoM growth rate
├─ Recurring revenue (subscriptions)
├─ One-time revenue (purchases)
└─ Projection to annual target
```

This framework ensures KataGame can systematically generate revenue across all MVP phases!
